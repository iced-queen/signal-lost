Connecting…
YOUR TWO-PERSON CREW

Good communication saves the night.

  1. Start a voice call or sit together. Voice chat isn't built into the game.
  2. Select TX / HOST, enter your call sign, and open a private channel. Send your partner its link or code. They use RX / PARTNER to connect. Keep your screens to yourselves.
  3. The operator sees the console. The signal analyst sees the manual. Describe what you see; work out the instructions together.
  4. Both select Ready. The host starts the mission. Restore every module before the timer or strike limit runs out.
  5. Swap roles and try another transmission. Quiet frequency has no timer.

Colors also have text labels. All controls work with touch or keyboard. A disconnected crew member pauses the mission until they return.
